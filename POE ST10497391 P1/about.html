About Nuru Creative Print & Design

Nuru Creative Print & Design is a hypothetical South African small business created for the WEDE502POE website project.

The organisation provides affordable creative design, printing and promotional support to entrepreneurs, community organisations and individuals.

Our Mission

Our Mission is to make professional visual communication more accessible by combining practical design, reliable printingand customer-focused services.

Our Vision

Our vision is to become a trusted creative partner for emerging South African brands, entrepreneursand community organisations

Our Core Vaulues

  • Creativity
  • Reliability
  • Afforability
  • customer Service
  • Community Growth
  • Professionalism

Our Customers

Nuru Creative Print & Design primarily serves small business owners, students, community groups, event organisers and individuals looking for practical creative solutions.
